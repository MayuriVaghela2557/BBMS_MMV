Our story

Built by donors, run for patients

LifeLine Blood Bank began as a single collection center and has grown into a regional network linking donors, mobile camps, testing labs, and partner hospitals. Every process — from registration to delivery — is designed around one goal: no request should ever go unmet.

Today we coordinate with hundreds of hospitals and thousands of donors to keep a steady, safe blood supply available around the clock.

  • Licensed medical staff at every collection point
  • Rigorous screening and cold-chain storage standards
  • 24/7 emergency request coordination
What drives us

Mission, Vision & Objectives

Our Mission

To make safe blood accessible to every patient in need, by connecting willing donors with hospitals through a fast, transparent system.

Our Vision

A future where blood shortages never delay treatment, supported by an informed and engaged donor community.

Our Objectives

Expand donor registration, maintain rigorous safety standards, and shorten response time between a request and a matched unit.

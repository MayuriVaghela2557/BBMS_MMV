Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about donating, requesting, and the safety standards behind every unit of blood.

Home FAQ
Most healthy adults aged 18 to 65, weighing at least 50kg, can donate. A quick health screening at the center will confirm your eligibility on the day.
Whole blood donors can typically donate every 56 days. Platelet donors may be eligible to donate more frequently — our staff will advise you based on your last donation.
Yes. All equipment is sterile and single-use, and every donation is supervised by licensed medical staff following strict safety protocols.
The donation itself takes about 8 to 10 minutes, though the full visit including registration and a short rest period usually takes 30 to 45 minutes.
Eat a healthy meal, stay well hydrated, and get a good night's sleep before your appointment. Avoid fatty foods right before donating.
Yes. Use the Request Blood form with the patient's details, hospital, and required blood group, and mark the emergency level so our team can prioritize accordingly.
Most donors feel completely normal afterward. We recommend resting for a few minutes, having a snack, and avoiding strenuous activity for the rest of the day.
Every donation is tested in our laboratory to confirm blood type and screen for safety before it's added to the available inventory.
